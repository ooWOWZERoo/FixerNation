1. The short version

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers. The site has no third-party advertising trackers. We collect what we need to run the program, answer you, and process orders, and the sections below say exactly what that is.

2. What we collect and why

  • What you tell us. Your name, email, school or organization, and the message in any form you fill in, including the contact form, Get help, a quote request, an affiliate application, a newsletter signup and a privacy request. We use it to reply and to set up what you asked for.
  • Account details. Your name, email, role, and a password that we keep only as a salted hash, so nobody here can read it. If you use "Continue with Microsoft," we keep the identifier Microsoft gives us so we recognize you next time.
  • Orders. Your name, email, school, what you bought, seat counts, invoices and purchase order numbers. Your card number goes to Stripe and never reaches us.
  • Support requests. What you write and the details needed to fix the problem. We delete requests two years after they come in.
  • Email activity. For newsletters, campaigns and Morning Boost, we record whether a message was opened and which links were clicked, so we can see what is useful. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Error reports. When a page breaks in your browser, it sends a short report of what failed and where. It has no IP address, no name or email, and nothing you typed into a form. We delete these reports after 30 days.
  • Anonymous visit data. Which pages get viewed and in what order, tied to a random ID that lives only in your browser tab and disappears when you close it. It carries no name, email or IP address, and you can turn it off on the Privacy Choices page.
  • Community profiles. If you check the consent box, your name, email, school and bio are shown to other verified Community members. You can uncheck the box at any time.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our server sees your IP address when your browser connects, and our hosting provider keeps ordinary server logs. We use IP addresses to keep the site safe, for example to slow down repeated failed sign-ins. We don't attach them to visit data or error reports.

Student information is covered on the Student Data Privacy page.

3. Cookies and browser storage

We use a few small pieces of browser storage, and none of them is for advertising:

  • Sign-in cookies that keep you logged in. They are marked secure, and page scripts can't read them.
  • A referral cookie, kept for 90 days, if you arrive through an affiliate's link. It lets us credit that affiliate if you buy.
  • Your shopping cart and display preferences, stored in your own browser.

Some pages embed videos from YouTube or Vimeo. When one of those loads, that company may set its own cookies and sees your IP address, under its own policy. Student lesson pages only link out to those videos.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the companies that help us run FNE, and only what each one needs:

  • Hosting.com hosts the site, database, uploaded files and mail server, in the United States.
  • Stripe processes card payments and receives the buyer's name, email and order details.
  • Microsoft receives your sign-in only if you choose "Continue with Microsoft."
  • Cloudinary hosts our how-to videos and sees your IP address and browser details when you play one.
  • YouTube, Vimeo and Unsplash see your IP address when a page that uses them loads.

Schools and districts can see information about the teachers and students in their own accounts. We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

5. How long we keep information

Support requests are deleted after two years, and error reports after 30 days. We keep account and order records while your account or license is active and as long as we need them for taxes and bookkeeping. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, and we will tell you if something has to be kept. Student data follows the rules on the Student Data Privacy page.

6. Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom. Morning Boost has its own unsubscribe link.
  • Turn off anonymous visit tracking on the Privacy Choices page.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to opt out of any sale or sharing, with the request form on that same page. We reply by email.
  • Withdraw Community consent by unchecking the box on your profile.

7. Security

The site uses HTTPS everywhere, passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and each kind of user (student, teacher, parent, school admin, district admin and FNE staff) has its own sign-in and sees only what that role needs. No system is perfectly secure, so if you think something has gone wrong, tell us through Get help.

8. Children

Children don't create FNE accounts on their own. Students use FNE through a classroom account their teacher sets up, and our newsletter and adult accounts are for adults. The Student Data Privacy page explains how student information is handled, including FERPA and COPPA.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page and post a notice on the site for significant changes.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your information can go through the Privacy Choices form, the Contact page, or Get help.

Want to see or delete your information?

Send a privacy request and we will reply by email.
