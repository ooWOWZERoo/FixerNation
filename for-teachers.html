Fixer Nation Education (FNE) puts a complete 2D SEL lesson in your hands every school morning. 2D stands for digital delivery: social-emotional learning designed for flexible, modern instruction. Each lesson includes video, discussion guide, handout, interactive quiz, and reflection. Deliver it through the FNE Digital Classroom, play it on your classroom display, or print the materials — your call. No planning required. You bring the classroom.

No Prep Required

Open the morning with a video and a prompt.

The materials guide the lesson while you lead the conversation. Every script, discussion question, handout, and quiz is already written and formatted. You focus on your students; FNE handles the content.

Every lesson follows the same five-step Issues-to-Answers structure: Recognize the Issue, Understand the Impact, Consider Possible Answers, Choose a Responsible Response, and Reflect and Reinforce. Once students know the routine, they engage without being led step by step, and you spend your time on conversation, not logistics.

Five to fifteen minutes every morning. Consistent daily practice. The skills that accumulate over a school year are the skills that last.
