How to Lie and Get Away With It Every Time

$18.99
In Stock

This Book Is Based On True Story

From childhood fibs to workplace misdirection, How to Lie and Get Away With It Every Time opens your eyes to the human tendency to bend truth. Anthony J. Placito shows readers how to notice signs of dishonesty and respond wisely. The book emphasizes awareness, ethics, and emotional intelligence — helping you protect yourself from manipulation while cultivating trust and understanding.

Life is full of subtle manipulation, white lies, and hidden truths. This book teaches you to recognize deception without losing trust in people, offering insights into human behavior that show how honesty and awareness can coexist.

One of the strongest messages in this book is that awareness does not require bitterness. Readers learn how to recognize patterns of manipulation while still understanding human fear and insecurity — protecting themselves while remaining empathetic, and maintaining healthy boundaries without becoming closed off or cynical.
