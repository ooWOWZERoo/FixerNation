# C5  Record your Portrait of a Graduate (part 1)  (page: district-admin-standards.html)
# Record as: sales-demo district administrator. The demo district is never public, so nothing recorded here goes live.
# Use made-up demo standards only. NEVER enter or show a real district's framework unless it is that district's own official document.
[SCREEN] Open Portrait of a Graduate.
[SAY] Many districts have a Portrait of a Graduate, or their own social and emotional learning standards. In this video you'll record yours, so we can show how Fixer Nation lessons connect to it.
[SCREEN] The disclaimer at the top.
[SAY] Before you start, read the note at the top of the page. The connections shown here are Fixer Nation's own suggested matches. They don't mean your district, your state, or CASEL has endorsed our program.
[SCREEN] Select Add a framework.
[SAY] Select Add a framework. Give it a name, and choose a type: a Portrait of a Graduate, or social and emotional learning standards. Choose your state, and enter who published it, which starts out as your district's name.
[SAY] Then paste the link to your district's official page for it, and the date you checked it. Every framework needs both. That's how we confirm it's the real thing. Year adopted and grade span are optional.
[SAY] The type can't be changed after you save, so pick carefully. You can have up to three frameworks, and each can hold up to sixty standards.
[SCREEN] Open the framework, select Add a standard.
[SAY] Now add the standards. Select Add a standard. Enter its code, if it has one, a short title, and the grade band if it applies. Then write a short summary, up to six hundred characters, in your own words.
[SAY] Please don't copy long passages from your district's document. A short summary is enough, and the link to your official page covers the rest. If the standard lines up with a CASEL competency, you can choose it.
[SCREEN] Edit and delete a standard.
[SAY] You can edit or delete a standard at any time. In the next video, you'll suggest connections and send the framework to us for review.
