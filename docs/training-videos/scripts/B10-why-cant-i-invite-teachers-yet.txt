[SAY] You've signed in, you press Invite Teacher, and it won't let you send. This video explains the three most common reasons, and what to do about each.
[SCREEN] The payment pending banner.
[SAY] The most common reason is a banner that says License payment pending. Invitations can't be sent until your school's invoice is marked paid.

[SCREEN] The dashboard seat warning.
[SAY] The second reason is that your seats are full. Pending invitations count as used seats, so if you've invited as many people as you have seats, there's no room for more. Revoke an invitation you don't need, or email us to add seats.
[SCREEN] A trial license's Branding or Organization message.
[SAY] The third is a trial license. A trial license may not have a school domain attached yet, so a few features, such as branding and safety alert recipients, will say they aren't set up yet. When your school moves to a full license, we'll finish that setup for you.
