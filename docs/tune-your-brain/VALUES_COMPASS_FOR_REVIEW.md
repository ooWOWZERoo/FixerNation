# Values Compass (grades 6-8): the 12 finished stories, for your read

Written 2026-11-01 from the outline you approved. **Held: not committed or deployed until you have read it.** Each run plays 5 of these 12 stories, narrated. Step one: "What do you notice in this story?" (the student picks what really happens; one choice is not in the story). Step two: "Which values could this show? You can pick more than one." Nothing is scored right or wrong, and no value is ranked. Then a private check, never logged, sent or saved.

**What the tests enforce:** 3 true cues and 1 untrue cue per story; 3 values that fit and 1 that fits less, with a gentle note; no two stories share the same set of values; no dashes; fictional names; the private check keeps its order and its answers are never logged (a test reads the engine to confirm).

**Please check:** the "fits less" notes (the student reads these), story 9 (a dare to prank a teacher) and story 10 (resting after a busy week), and the private-check wording.

## The private check (after every story)

- **"Does this matter to you?"** Yes / A little / Not sure
- **"Where does it show up in your life?"** At school / At home / With friends / Somewhere else / Not sure
- Closing: "What you care about can change as you grow. No one else sees what you picked."
- Screen-reader note: "Your answers here are private and are not saved."
- Summary label: "Times you thought about your own values"

## 1

💬 Rin's group chat keeps making jokes about a quiet classmate. Rin writes, "I'm out," and leaves the chat. She tells one friend why, and she keeps saying hi to the classmate in the hallway.

**What do you notice?**
- She tells the group that she is out.
- She joins in on the jokes.  *(not in the story)*
- She explains it once to a friend.
- She keeps being friendly to the classmate.

**Which values could this show?**
- Fairness
- Courage
- Respect
- Popularity  *(fits less; the student reads: "Rin gave up being popular in this story, so this one fits less.")*

**Closing line:** Rin acted on what she thought was fair, and it took courage.

## 2

**What do you notice?**

- She copies her friend's answers.  *(not in the story)*

**Closing line:** Dara's choice shows more than one value at once.

## 3
