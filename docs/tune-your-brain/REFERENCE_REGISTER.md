# Tune Your Brain — External Reference Register

**Purpose:** This is a pre-implementation reference register for the proposed expansion of FNE's "Brain Games" / "Tune Your Brain" feature (6 cognitive-training games) into an 18-experience, 4-age-band, 5-domain learning platform. It records every external, authoritative source that should inform or constrain that build, with URLs actually fetched (via WebFetch/WebSearch) on the access date below — not paraphrased from training-data memory.

**Compiled by:** Claude Code, for John Shaw (john.shaw@fullsteam.com)
**Access date for all rows below:** 2026-09-16 (unless a different access attempt date is noted)

**Important disclaimer:** This register documents sources that are *relevant to* or *should inform* the Tune Your Brain blueprint. Listing a source here is not a claim that FNE is compliant with, certified by, or endorsed by that organization, law, or standard. Several items below are proposed/draft/non-binding or apply only to specific entity types (e.g., state/local government) and may not apply to FNE directly — this is called out per row. Compliance determinations require legal/compliance review, not this document.
