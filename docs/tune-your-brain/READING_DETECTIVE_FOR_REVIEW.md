## 1 (existing) · inference

1. Mia looked everywhere for her lunchbox before the bell rang.
2. She remembered eating outside at the picnic tables that morning.
3. When she checked her backpack, it smelled like fresh-cut grass.  **(proving sentence)**

*What most likely happened to Mia’s lunchbox?*

- **RIGHT:** She probably left it at the picnic tables outside.
- Someone must have taken it from her locker at school.
- She forgot to bring a lunchbox from home that day.

**Why (shown to the child):** The grass smell is the clue. It points back outside, not to her locker.

## 2 (existing) · inference

1. A new student, Jordan, sat alone at the lunch table.
2. Jordan kept looking around at the other tables without saying anything.
3. When a classmate waved, Jordan smiled and waved back right away.  **(proving sentence)**

*How does Jordan most likely feel?*

- **RIGHT:** Nervous, but hopeful about making friends.
- Bored, and not interested in meeting anyone new.
- Upset with the other students for ignoring him.

**Why (shown to the child):** Jordan waves back right away. That looks hopeful, not uninterested or angry.

## 3 (existing) · main idea

1. Ms. Patel found chalk footprints from the art room to the library.  **(proving sentence)**
2. The footprints stopped right at the librarian’s desk.
3. A half-finished chalk drawing sat on the art room table, still wet.

*What is the main idea of this passage?*

- **RIGHT:** A trail of clues shows where someone went.
- Chalk drawings are always messy to clean up.
- The library is usually a very quiet place.

**Why (shown to the child):** The footprints are a trail. A trail shows where someone went.

## 4 (existing) · prediction

1. The class had planned a picnic for recess all week.
2. By lunchtime, thick gray clouds came in and the wind picked up.  **(proving sentence)**
3. The teacher checked the weather app and frowned.

*What will most likely happen next?*

- **RIGHT:** The picnic will probably be moved inside.
- The picnic will happen outside exactly as planned.
- School will be canceled for the whole day.

**Why (shown to the child):** Gray clouds, strong wind and a frown all point one way. The plan will change.

## 5 (new) · main idea

1. Every Saturday morning, Mr. Ruiz opens the community garden gate at eight.
2. Kids carry watering cans while grown-ups pass out gloves.  **(proving sentence)**
3. Last week the first tomatoes turned red, and everyone stopped to cheer.
4. By noon the beds are weeded and the tools are put away.

*What is the main idea of this passage?*

- **RIGHT:** Neighbors take care of the garden together.
- Tomatoes are the hardest plant to grow well.
- Mr. Ruiz is usually late opening the garden gate.

**Why (shown to the child):** Kids and grown-ups all help. Nothing says tomatoes are hard.

## 6 (new) · inference

1. Sam practiced his one line in the bathroom mirror all week.
2. On the night of the play, his hands shook behind the curtain.
3. When his turn came, he took a slow breath and walked out.
4. Afterward, he grinned and asked his mom for ice cream.  **(proving sentence)**

*How does Sam most likely feel after the play?*

- **RIGHT:** Proud that he got through his part.
- Sad that the play is finally over.
- Angry that he only had one line to say.

**Why (shown to the child):** Sam grins and wants to celebrate. He feels proud.

## 7 (new) · cause and effect

1. It rained all morning, so the school field was covered in puddles.
2. At recess, Ms. Lee pointed at the muddy grass and handed out board games.  **(proving sentence)**
3. Some kids groaned, but Tia found a friend to play checkers with.

*Why did Ms. Lee hand out board games at recess?*

- **RIGHT:** The field was too wet and muddy to play on.
- The class had lost all of the playground balls.
- It was the school’s special game day for everyone.

**Why (shown to the child):** She points at the muddy grass, then hands out games. The field is too wet.

## 8 (new) · vocabulary in context

1. The old wooden bridge was fragile.
2. Cars were not allowed, and people crossed one at a time.
3. A sign at the entrance said, “Please walk slowly and be gentle.”  **(proving sentence)**

*What does the word fragile most likely mean?*

- **RIGHT:** Easy to break or damage
- Longer than other bridges
- Painted bright colors

**Why (shown to the child):** The sign says to be gentle. You are gentle with things that break.

## 9 (new) · main idea

1. A honeybee visits hundreds of flowers in one day.
2. As it drinks nectar, yellow dust called pollen sticks to its legs.
3. At the next flower, pollen rubs off and helps it make seeds.  **(proving sentence)**
4. Many fruits and vegetables we eat grow because bees do this.

*What is the main idea of this passage?*

- **RIGHT:** Bees help plants grow by carrying pollen.
- Honeybees only visit flowers that are yellow.
- Flowers make all of their seeds in winter.

**Why (shown to the child):** Pollen rubs off at the next flower. That helps plants grow.

## 10 (new) · prediction

1. Maya packed her swim bag and put her goggles on top.
2. Her brother Leo asked if she was ready for the meet.
3. She nodded, but she kept checking the clock by the door.
4. The bus to the pool would leave in two minutes.  **(proving sentence)**

*What will Maya most likely do next?*

- **RIGHT:** Rush out the door to catch the bus.
- Unpack her bag and stay home today.
- Sit down and start a game with Leo.

**Why (shown to the child):** The bus leaves soon and she keeps checking the clock.

## 11 (new) · inference

1. The library cat, Biscuit, sleeps in the sunny window every afternoon.
2. He lets kids pet him, but only if they sit quietly.
3. One day a loud group ran in and Biscuit hid.  **(proving sentence)**
4. The next day he came back out when the room was calm.

*What can you tell about Biscuit?*

- **RIGHT:** He feels safest in a quiet room.
- He does not like any children at all.
- He only ever sleeps during the night.

**Why (shown to the child):** He hides when it is loud and returns when it is calm.

## 12 (new) · character traits

1. Jin’s little sister dropped her ice cream cone on the sidewalk.
2. She started to cry.
3. Jin looked at his own cone, then at her wobbling chin.
4. He held it out and said, “You can have mine.”  **(proving sentence)**

*Which words best describe Jin?*

- **RIGHT:** Kind and willing to share
- Jealous of his little sister
- Bored with his ice cream cone

**Why (shown to the child):** He gives up his own cone to cheer her up. That is kind and generous.
