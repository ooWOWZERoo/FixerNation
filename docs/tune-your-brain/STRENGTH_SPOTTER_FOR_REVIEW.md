# Strength Spotter (grades 3-5): the 12 finished stories, for your read

Written 2026-11-01 from the outline you approved. **Held: not committed or deployed until you have read it.** Each run plays 5 of these 12 stories, narrated. Step one: "What do you notice in this story?" (the child picks what really happens; one choice is not in the story). Step two: "Which strengths could this show? You can pick more than one." Nothing is scored right or wrong. Then a private check, never logged, sent or saved.

**One change from the outline:** story 10 (Ben) uses "Being polite" as its third strength instead of "Honesty", because Ben says thank you and honesty does not fit that story.

**What the tests enforce:** 3 true cues and 1 untrue cue per story; 3 strengths that fit and 1 that fits less, with a gentle note; no two stories share the same set of strengths; no dashes; fictional names; the private check keeps its order and its answers are never logged (a test reads the engine to confirm). The engine got one small change, optional wording for the prompts; a test confirms Feelings Detective's wording is untouched.

**Please check:** the "fits less" notes (the child reads these), story 8 (Teo and the language at home), and the private-check wording at the end.

## 1

📿 Maya's little brother has a necklace knotted into a tight tangle. Maya sits down with him and works on it. It takes ten minutes, and she stays calm the whole time.

**What do you notice?**
- She sits down and works slowly.
- She gives up and puts it in a drawer.  *(not in the story)*
- She keeps trying when the knot will not come loose.
- She talks in a calm voice.

**Which strengths could this show?**
- Patience
- Persistence
- Caring
- Being the fastest  *(fits less; the child reads: "Maya took her time. Speed is not what the story is about.")*

**Closing line:** Maya shows more than one strength at once. That is how strengths usually work.

## 2

💧 Diego sees a puddle by the playground in the morning. By afternoon it is gone. He wonders where the water went, so he asks his teacher and writes a note in his notebook.

**What do you notice?**
- He checks the puddle again after school.
- He asks his teacher a question.
- He stomps through the puddle and runs off.  *(not in the story)*
- He writes what he sees in his notebook.

**Which strengths could this show?**
- Curiosity
- Noticing
- Careful thinking
- Being loud  *(fits less; the child reads: "Nothing in the story is loud. Diego is quiet and careful, so this one fits less.")*

**Closing line:** Diego wonders, notices and thinks carefully, all in one morning.

## 3

## 4

🧱 At recess, Jo's block tower falls over, and Jo looks upset. Sam says, "That tower was so tall it touched the clouds!" Jo laughs, and then they start building again.

**What do you notice?**
- Sam jokes about the tower, not about Jo.
- Jo laughs.
- Sam laughs at Jo.  *(not in the story)*
- They build the tower again together.

**Which strengths could this show?**
- Humor
- Kindness
- Encouraging others
- Showing off  *(fits less; the child reads: "Sam's joke was meant to help Jo feel better, so this one fits less.")*

**Closing line:** Humor can be a way to be kind. Sam used it to cheer Jo up.

## 5

🏀 Zoe wants to make a free throw in basketball. For three weeks she practices every day after school, and she misses a lot. One day she tries a new way to hold the ball, and it goes in.

**What do you notice?**
- She practices every day.
- She quits after the first week.  *(not in the story)*
- She keeps going after she misses.
- She tries a new way to hold the ball.

**Which strengths could this show?**
- Persistence
- Hard work
- Hope
- Luck  *(fits less; the child reads: "Zoe made the shot after weeks of practice, so luck is only a small part of this story.")*

**Closing line:** Zoe kept trying, and she kept believing it could work.

## 6

**What do you notice?**

- He tells Lee to stop being sad.  *(not in the story)*

**Which strengths could this show?**

- Caring

- Fixing it fast  *(fits less; the child reads: "Omar did not rush to fix anything. He listened first, so this one fits less.")*

**Closing line:** Listening is a strength, and so is giving a friend time.

## 7

## 10

**Closing line:** Ben did something hard, and he did it politely.

## 12
