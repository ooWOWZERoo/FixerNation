# Release 131: Discover games, more rounds (for your review)

Written 2026-11-06. Claude drafted every new round; Claude cannot sign off SEL content, so please read them. Each audio-led game had 4 rounds (Same and Different 12, Feelings Detective 4); the pools are now 18, 18, 18, 24 and 12, and each play draws 5 (Feelings Detective 4). The right answer is marked with a star. Every round also now has a short spoken explanation. The voice for every line is recorded Ava (Premium).

What to check: is the right answer clearly the right one for a 5 to 7 year old, are the wrong answers clearly wrong without being shaming, and does anything touch a topic you want left out.

## Calm Down Corner (What helps you feel calm?) (18 rounds)

1. Your tower fell down. You feel mad. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Deep breaths** / Yell / Knock more down  
   _Slow breaths help your body settle down._
2. A puzzle piece will not fit. You feel grumpy. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Count to five** / Throw it / Give up  
   _Counting gives your mad feeling time to shrink._
3. Someone took your turn on the swing. You feel upset. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Ask a grown-up** / Push and cry / Keep it inside  
   _A grown-up can help when you feel stuck._
4. You spilled your juice. Your face feels hot. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Calm spot** / Yell "Not fair" / Kick something  
   _A calm spot helps your body cool down._
5. Your ice cream fell on the ground. You feel sad and mad. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Hug your stuffy** / Stomp and scream / Grab a friend's  
   _Something soft to hug helps big feelings settle._
6. It is time to stop playing. You wanted more. You feel cross. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Count to ten** / Run away / Drop to the floor  
   _Counting slowly gives your feelings time to get smaller._
7. Your friend won the game. You wanted to win. You feel mad. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Say good game** / Say it is unfair / Knock the board  
   _Saying good game helps the mad feeling pass._
8. Thunder is booming. Your tummy feels tight. You feel scared. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Slow tummy breaths** / Scream loudly / Run outside  
   _Slow tummy breaths tell your body it is safe._
9. You cannot tie your shoe. You feel grumpy. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Breathe, then try** / Throw the shoe / Say you will never  
   _A calm breath helps your fingers try again._
10. Someone said your drawing is silly. You feel hurt. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Tell a teacher** / Say mean things / Rip it up  
   _Telling a grown-up helps when words hurt._
11. Your sister is very loud. You feel cross. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Quiet corner** / Shout louder / Slam the door  
   _A quiet corner gives your ears and feelings a rest._
12. You lost your favorite crayon. Your face is hot. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Sip some water** / Blame a friend / Break the others  
   _A cool drink helps your body slow down._
13. The line is long. You have to wait. You feel wiggly and cross. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Hum a quiet song** / Cut in line / Poke the kid ahead  
   _A quiet song gives waiting something to do._
14. Your friend cannot come over. You feel sad and cross. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Draw a picture** / Stay mad all day / Say mean things  
   _Drawing lets feelings out in a safe way._
15. You made a mistake on your paper. You feel upset. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Breathe, then fix** / Crumple it up / Say you are bad  
   _Mistakes help us learn. Take a breath, then fix it._
16. The room is very noisy. You feel shaky. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Ask for quiet** / Yell to be heard / Run out  
   _Asking for a quiet spot helps you feel steady._
17. It is bedtime. You are not done playing. You feel mad. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Cuddle and read** / Stomp around / Throw the toys  
   _A cozy story helps your body get ready to rest._
18. Your balloon popped. You feel sad and shocked. What helps you feel calm?  
   **★ Ask for a hug** / Pop another one / Hide away  
   _A hug can help sad feelings get smaller._

## Helper's Choice (What is the right thing to do?) (18 rounds)

1. You find a dollar on the floor. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Tell the teacher** / Keep it quiet / Into your bag  
   _The teacher can find who lost it._
2. You spilled paint on the table. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Tell and clean up** / Hide it / Blame someone  
   _Telling the truth and cleaning up fixes the problem._
3. You said you would feed the fish. You are playing. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Feed the fish** / Skip it / Get grumpy  
   _Keeping a promise shows you can be trusted._
4. Your friend says, "Say we both finished." You did not finish. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Tell the truth** / Go along / Get mad  
   _Telling the truth is right, even when it is hard._
5. You broke a classmate's pencil by accident. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Say sorry and tell** / Hide the pieces / Blame another kid  
   _Saying sorry fixes hurt feelings._
6. The teacher gave you extra stickers by mistake. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Tell the teacher** / Keep them / Tuck them away  
   _Being honest is right, even when keeping them is easy._
7. It is your turn to clean up the blocks. Friends want to play. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Clean up first** / Run off to play / Leave it  
   _Doing your job first is fair to everyone._
8. You see trash on the playground floor. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Use the bin** / Walk past it / Kick it away  
   _Caring for our space helps everyone._
9. A friend left their lunch box at the park. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Return it** / Eat the snack / Leave it there  
   _Giving it back is the honest thing to do._
10. You see a classmate taking something that is not theirs. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Tell a teacher** / Say nothing / Take one too  
   _Telling a teacher keeps things fair._
11. You do not understand the work. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Ask for help** / Pretend you do / Copy a friend  
   _Asking for help is how we learn._
12. You told a small lie. Now you feel bad. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Tell the truth** / Tell another lie / Hope it goes away  
   _The truth lets the bad feeling go._
13. A toy is broken in the classroom. You did not do it. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Tell the teacher** / Say nothing / Blame a friend  
   _Telling the teacher helps fix the toy._
14. A friend asks you to pick on a kid. You do not want to. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Say no thanks** / Do it / Go along quietly  
   _It takes courage to say no to something unkind._
15. A kid cuts in front of you in line. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Say it nicely** / Shove them / Let everyone cut  
   _Using kind words keeps the line fair._
16. You borrowed a book. It is time to give it back. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Give it back** / Keep it longer / Say you lost it  
   _Returning what you borrow keeps trust strong._
17. Your team lost the game. Someone is crying. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Say good game** / Tease them / Leave the team  
   _Being a good sport is the right thing to do._
18. You see a hurt bird on the path. What is the right thing to do?  
   **★ Tell a grown-up** / Poke it / Run past  
   _A grown-up knows how to help a hurt animal._

## Same and Different (24 rounds)

1. Ana loves soccer. She has a dog. Ben loves drawing. He has a dog too. What is the same?  
   **★ A dog** / Soccer / Drawing  
   _Friends are the same in some ways and different in others. Both are okay._
2. Mia wears glasses. Leo does not. They both like red. What is different?  
   **★ Glasses** / Red / Same class  
   _Glasses help Mia see. Being different is okay, and friends can still play together._
3. Kofi eats rice. Lena eats a sandwich. They both have a little sister. What is the same?  
   **★ Little sister** / Eating rice / Sandwiches  
   _Their lunches are different, and having a little sister is the same._
4. Sam is new. He speaks Spanish at home. Ana wants to play with him. What could Ana do?  
   **★ Ask for a word** / Wait and see / Play without him  
   _You can play with a friend who speaks another language. You learn words from each other._
5. Nina has noodles. Omar has bread and cheese. They sit at the same table. What is different?  
   **★ Their lunches** / Being hungry / The same table  
   _People eat different foods, and that is okay. You can still share a table._
6. Leo uses a wheelchair. His class plays tag. What could the class do?  
   **★ Change the game** / Let Leo watch / Play as usual  
   _Games can change so that everyone can play. Everybody belongs._
7. Zuri has braids. Eli has short hair. They both love to dance. What is the same?  
   **★ Dancing** / Braids / Short hair  
   _Hair can look different, and friends can love the same things._
8. Priya lives with her grandma and dad. Tom lives with his two moms. Both have people who love them. What is different?  
   **★ Who they live with** / People who love them / Having a family  
   _Families look different from each other. Every family can love a child._
9. Ben likes quiet games. Ana likes loud, running games. Ana wants to play with Ben. What could Ana do?  
   **★ A quiet game** / Be much louder / Play loud alone  
   _Friends can try one game they like, then one the other likes._
10. Hana grew up in a big city. Raul grew up in a small town. Both feel nervous on the first day. What is the same?  
   **★ Feeling nervous** / Big city / Small town  
   _Feelings can be the same even when the places are different._
11. Ada's family puts candles on a cake. Jin's family eats long noodles. Both have a birthday and a treat. What is different?  
   **★ Candles or noodles** / Having a birthday / A special treat  
   _Families celebrate in different ways, and every way is special._
12. Maya wants blocks. Dev wants tag. They want to play together. What is a kind way to decide?  
   **★ Take turns** / Pick your favorite / Stop playing  
   _Taking turns is a kind way to play when friends like different things._
13. Ria has a cat. Tom has a fish. They both love animals. What is the same?  
   **★ Animals** / Ria's cat / Tom's fish  
   _Friends can have different pets and still love animals._
14. Lila walks to school. Max rides a bus. They both get there at nine. What is different?  
   **★ How they get there** / The time / The school  
   _There is more than one way to get to school._
15. Eva likes pizza. Noah likes tacos. They both like eating with friends. What is the same?  
   **★ Eating with friends** / Pizza / Tacos  
   _Different favorite foods can still be shared at one table._
16. Jay is tall. Sue is short. They both run fast. What is different?  
   **★ How tall** / Running fast / Having feet  
   _Bodies come in many sizes, and all are great for running._
17. Pia speaks Tagalog at home. Ben speaks English. They both love story time. What is the same?  
   **★ Loving story time** / Home words / Home country  
   _Stories connect us, in any language._
18. Ali likes rain. Joy likes sun. They both like to play outside. What is the same?  
   **★ Outdoor play** / Splashing in rain / Joy's sunny days  
   _You can like different weather and still play together._
19. Rosa is shy. Pete is loud. They both want a friend. What could they do?  
   **★ A game for both** / Play alone / Be loud  
   _Shy and loud friends can play together._
20. Mo has a big family. Kim has a small family. They both love family time. What is different?  
   **★ How many people** / Loving family time / Having a family  
   _Families come in all sizes, and love is the same._
21. Lena paints. Carlos plays music. They both want to share their art. What could they do?  
   **★ Share together** / Hide it away / Say mine is best  
   _Different talents are fun to share._
22. Ivy writes with her left hand. Dan writes with his right. They both write their names. What is different?  
   **★ Which hand** / Writing names / Writing  
   _There are many right ways to write._
23. Tess has a cast on her arm. Ron does not. They both want to play. What could they do?  
   **★ A gentle game** / Play a rough game / Skip Tess  
   _We can change the game so everyone joins in._
24. Uma likes loud music. Abe likes quiet music. They share a room. What could they do?  
   **★ Take turns** / Play it louder / Say turn it off  
   _Taking turns helps two people share a space._
