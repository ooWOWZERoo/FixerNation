# Feelings Detective "Your turn": wording and recorded clips for your read (Release 139)

Written 2026-11-06 by Claude. **Built, tested and played in a browser, but not committed or deployed.** This is the third and last of the Self-Awareness finales you approved (`docs/reference/own-self-steps-outlines.md`, section 4). Strength Spotter and Values Compass went out in Release 138. Generated from the page, so it matches what a child sees and hears.

**What changed in the game:** 3 stories per run instead of 4. After each story one tap, "Has this happened to you?" (Yes / No / Not sure), and no second question or closing line. After the last story: **Your turn**, three questions about the child, then a feeling card read aloud. Nothing the child picks is logged, sent or saved; there is always an "I'm not sure" answer; no reading is needed.

**Please do two things:** (1) read the lines below; (2) **listen to the 41 recorded lines** in `docs/tune-your-brain/feelings-detective-your-turn-audition.html` (open the file in a browser on your Mac). 28 of the 41 are new clips; the rest already existed. I cannot hear them, only check their length, so I need your ears for speed, stress and any unclear word (the sounds of "Calm", "I'm not sure" and the short feeling cards are the ones I would listen to first).

## Your turn

Intro (read aloud): "Now it is your turn. Only you see what you pick."

### Step 1: how do you feel right now? (pick one or two)

Prompt (read aloud): "How do you feel right now? Pick one or two." Button: "I picked these"

- 😊 Happy → card: "I feel happy."
- 😢 Sad → card: "I feel sad."
- 😡 Angry → card: "I feel angry."
- 😨 Scared → card: "I feel scared."
- 😟 Nervous → card: "I feel nervous."
- 🤩 Excited → card: "I feel excited."
- 😎 Proud → card: "I feel proud."
- 😌 Calm → card: "I feel calm."
- I'm not sure (always available)

### Step 2: where do you feel it in your body?

Prompt: "Where do you feel it in your body?"

- My tummy → card: "I feel it in my tummy."
- My chest → card: "I feel it in my chest."
- My face → card: "I feel it in my face."
- My hands → card: "I feel it in my hands."
- Somewhere else → card: "I feel it somewhere else."
- I'm not sure

### Step 3: what helps?

Prompt: "What helps you with this feeling? Pick one."

- 🌬️ Slow breaths → card: "Slow breaths help me."
- 🗣️ Tell a grown-up → card: "Telling a grown-up helps me."
- 🏃 Move around → card: "Moving around helps me."
- 🤫 Sit somewhere quiet → card: "Sitting somewhere quiet helps me."
- I'm not sure

### The feeling card

Heading: "My feeling card". One line for each feeling picked (for example "I feel happy." and "I feel nervous."), then the body line, then the helper line. Each line is read aloud as its own recorded clip, then the closing:

- Closing: "Only you see this card. Grown-ups you trust can help with big feelings."
- If the child answered "I'm not sure" to everything: "That is okay. Feelings can be hard to name."
- Button: "Finish"

## Also fixed in this release

Tapping a "confirm", "Continue" or "Finish" button twice (very likely for a five-year-old) used to start the next screen twice, and a leftover screen could cover Your turn. I found it while testing this game. The buttons now ignore a second tap in all scenario-choice games, and a test covers it.
