This page explains the student experience for teachers, parents, and school administrators evaluating FNE.

The Student Flow

What students do each morning

The student experience follows the same four-part structure every day — familiar enough to build routine, fresh enough to stay engaging.

1

Watch a Short Video

Under five minutes. A real-life scenario built around a character, resilience, or decision-making theme.

2

Reflect on the Issue

Students write a short reflection connecting the lesson's issue to their own experience or perspective.

3

Complete the Quiz

A short comprehension quiz reinforces the lesson and gives teachers real-time data on understanding.

4

Set a Personal Goal

Students identify one thing they'll do or practice based on the day's lesson — building the habit of intentional growth.

The Student Portal

A simple, focused student experience.

Students join their class using a code from their teacher, then have immediate access to their assigned lessons and personal history.

🔑

Join via Class Code

No separate account creation required for students under teacher supervision. Join instantly with a teacher-provided code.

📚

Access Assigned Lessons

Students see only the lessons their teacher has assigned — a focused, distraction-free interface.

📊

Track Personal Goals

Students can review past goals they've set and see their history of completed lessons over time.

✍️

Submit Reflections

Written reflections are saved and visible to the assigning teacher — no public sharing, no social feed.

Privacy First — Always

Student accounts contain no advertising, no public profiles, and no social networking. Student data is never sold. Reflections are visible only to the assigning teacher and school administrator.
