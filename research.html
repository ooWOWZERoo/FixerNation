CASEL Competency Alignment

How FNE content maps to the CASEL framework

Fixer Nation Education lessons map to the five core social-emotional competencies defined by CASEL (the Collaborative for Academic, Social, and Emotional Learning).

Self-Awareness

Recognizing one's own emotions, values, and identity

FNE lessons frequently ask students to recognize the emotional and situational context of an issue before responding, building the habit of self-assessment.

Self-Management

Regulating emotions, thoughts, and behaviors

The Issues-to-Answers method requires students to pause and consider options before acting, a structured practice in impulse regulation and intentional choice.

Social Awareness

Perspective-taking, empathy, and diversity

Many Morning Boost videos depict multi-character scenarios, asking students to consider perspectives beyond their own before selecting a responsible response.

Relationship Skills

Communication, teamwork, and conflict resolution

Lesson themes in communication, collaboration, and conflict resolution address CASEL's relationship skills competency directly and explicitly.

Responsible Decision-Making

Ethical reasoning, consequences, and choices

The Issues-to-Answers method directly addresses this competency: evaluate the issue, consider possible answers, and choose a responsible response.

Alignment note: This competency mapping represents FNE's thematic alignment to CASEL's framework, not a formal review, endorsement, or certification by CASEL. CASEL's CARES guide (Collaborating States Initiative) identifies specific criteria for formally reviewed programs, which FNE has not yet pursued. For schools requiring formally reviewed programs under ESSA evidence standards, see the CASEL CARES Guide.

Measurement Transparency

What FNE measures and what it doesn't yet claim

What FNE tracks

  • Lesson completion rates by student and classroom
  • Quiz scores per lesson
  • Reflection submissions (text, not sentiment-scored)
  • Goal-setting entries by students
  • Teacher adoption and lesson-assignment frequency
  • Classroom-level engagement over time

These are engagement measures: they describe participation and usage, not validated assessments of social-emotional development or behavioral change.

What FNE does not yet claim

  • Published outcome studies on student SEL competency change
  • Randomized control trial (RCT) data
  • Academic achievement impact data
  • Behavioral reduction data (suspensions, incidents)
  • Validated SEL assessment scores (e.g., DESSA, SSIS)
  • CASEL CARES designation or ESSA evidence tier

What we're working toward

FNE is designing a pilot evaluation framework to begin collecting outcome data in partner schools. Our goal is to move from engagement metrics to measurable evidence of SEL habit formation. We'll be clear about that distinction until the evidence catches up.

If your school or district requires ESSA-aligned evidence tiers for procurement, we recommend reviewing the CASEL CARES Guide for formally reviewed programs. Feel free to reach out if you have questions about where FNE fits within your district's requirements.
