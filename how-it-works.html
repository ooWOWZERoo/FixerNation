The Daily Platform

One fresh lesson. Every school morning.
Tracked and assessed.

Fixer Nation Education (FNE) delivers a new 2D SEL lesson every school morning. 2D stands for digital delivery: social-emotional learning designed for flexible, modern instruction. Each weekday lesson includes video, discussion materials, an interactive quiz, and a reflection activity. Teachers can deliver lessons entirely through the FNE Digital Classroom, present them on screen using existing classroom digital whiteboards or overhead displays, or download and print supporting materials to fit their classroom needs and teaching environment.

Our Instructional Approach

Every lesson follows the same five-step structure.

The Issues-to-Answers method is FNE's proprietary framework. It gives students a consistent mental structure for navigating real-life situations, starting with recognition and ending with reflection.

1

Recognize the Issue

Students identify the core challenge in the scenario before reacting. Awareness precedes response.

2

Understand the Impact

Students consider who is affected and how, building empathy and broader awareness of consequences.

3

Consider Possible Answers

Students generate multiple responses instead of defaulting to the first instinct, a structured decision-making practice.

4

Choose a Responsible Response

Students select and explain their best answer, practicing the habit of intentional, values-driven choice.

5

Reflect and Reinforce

Students connect the lesson to their own lives and set a personal goal, closing the loop from insight to action.

The Issues-to-Answers framework is FNE's proprietary instructional design. It has not been submitted for external validation or CASEL endorsement.

The Digital Classroom

Assign. Track. Report. All in one place.

Digital delivery means more than just a video. The FNE Digital Classroom is where the 2D part happens — teachers assign, students complete, and administrators monitor, all tracked automatically with nothing to print or manage manually.

For Teachers

  • Create one or more classrooms
  • Assign Morning Boost lessons by date
  • View each student's quiz scores and reflections
  • Track assignment completion in real time
  • Access the full lesson library at any time

For Students

  • Join a class with a teacher-provided code
  • Access assigned lessons from any device
  • Complete digital quizzes and submit reflections
  • Set and track personal goals tied to lessons
  • View lesson history and past results

For Administrators

  • View teacher adoption across the school
  • See classroom-level completion rates
  • Monitor school-wide engagement at a glance
  • No access to individual student PII or grades
  • Manage license and teacher seat assignments
