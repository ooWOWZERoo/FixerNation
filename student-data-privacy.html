Student Data Privacy

What we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Fixer Nation Education is designed for K–12 classrooms. Student data protection is a core design principle, not a compliance afterthought.

1. What data is collected from students

Student accounts in Fixer Nation Education are created under the supervision of a licensed teacher. The following information is collected from students:

  • First and last name (provided by the student during enrollment)
  • Email address (for account login; may be a school-issued address)
  • Lesson completion records (which lessons were completed, and when)
  • Quiz responses and scores for assigned lessons
  • Written reflections submitted in the student portal
  • Personal goals entered by the student
  • Class enrollment data (which Digital Classroom the student belongs to)

2. What data is NOT collected from students

FNE is explicitly designed to exclude the following categories of data from student accounts:

  • Location data (GPS, city, or address)
  • Biometric data of any kind
  • Social graph data (friends, followers, connections)
  • Behavioral tracking for advertising purposes
  • Public-facing profiles or user-generated content visible to other students
  • Health or wellness survey data
  • Racial, ethnic, or demographic categorization beyond what the school provides

3. FERPA

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) gives parents and eligible students rights over education records maintained by schools. When a school licenses Fixer Nation Education, student data collected through the platform constitutes education records subject to FERPA.

4. COPPA applicability

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) applies to online services that collect personal information from children under 13. FNE serves K–12 students, which includes children under 13.

7. Advertising policy

Fixer Nation Education does not display advertising to students. Student data is never sold to third parties. Student engagement data is not used for behavioral advertising or marketing profiling.
