Refunds and cancellations

Refunds and cancellations, in plain words.

We handle each request one at a time, and a person here reads every one. This page explains how to ask and what to expect.

1. How refunds work

Refunds are decided case by case. We don't have a fixed refund window or an automatic refund, so tell us what happened and we will talk it through with you.

2. How to ask

Send a request through Get help or the Contact page. It helps to include:

  • your school or district name
  • the email address on the order
  • the invoice or order number, if you have it
  • what went wrong and what you would like us to do

We reply by email, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday through Friday.

3. If you paid by card

When we approve a refund for a card payment, it goes back to the card that was charged, through Stripe. Your bank decides how long it takes to show up.

4. If you paid by purchase order

A purchase order license stays pending until we receive payment confirmation. If you need to cancel before you have paid, tell us and we will sort out the invoice with you. If you have already paid, we will work out the refund with your business office.

5. Cancelling a license

Licenses don't renew on their own, so you can simply let one end. It works through its end date, and then teacher seats switch off while classrooms, rosters and lesson picks are kept. If you want to stop before the end date and get money back, that is a refund request and we will look at it as described above.

6. Quotes, trials and demos

A quote is not a commitment. You aren't charged anything until you accept it, and the link stops working after its valid-until date. A quote for $0, such as a free trial, needs no payment and activates right away. Free trials and demos are arranged case by case.

7. Affiliate commissions

If an order that earned an affiliate commission is refunded, that commission can be reversed. The Affiliate program terms explain more.
