About FNE

The Fixer Nation story: why it belongs in every school morning.

Fixer Nation Education (FNE) was built from one conviction: social-emotional skills don't develop from a single lesson. They develop from daily practice. FNE puts that practice in the classroom, every school morning, across every classroom in the building.

One idea. One framework. One platform.

Fixer Nation Education started from a simple reframe: there are no problems in life, only issues and answers. That philosophy (that every challenge is solvable when you slow down, consider your options, and choose a responsible response) became the foundation of everything it has built.

What he saw, working with students and schools, was a gap. SEL programs existed, but they were periodic, delivered in units once a month, a few times a year. Students would encounter a lesson on resilience in October and not revisit it until spring. The skills that mattered most were getting the least consistent practice.

"Skills that matter outside the classroom only develop through consistent daily practice, not from a lesson students hear once."

FNE was built to close that gap. The Issues-to-Answers method (Recognize the Issue → Understand the Impact → Consider Possible Answers → Choose a Responsible Response → Reflect and Reinforce) became the structural scaffold for every Morning Boost lesson. Not a one-time curriculum. A daily routine.

That's the difference between a lesson a student heard and a habit a student has.

The FNE Mission

Every K–12 student deserves a daily chance to practice SEL, not just hear about it once. Every teacher deserves a tool that makes that possible without extra prep. Every school deserves a program that builds one shared language across every classroom, every morning.
