Anthony J. Placito is dedicated to helping individuals overcome obstacles, build confidence, and make smarter decisions. With a passion for personal growth and resilience, he shares powerful insights through his books: Your Past Doesn't Define You, Kill the Bully, Think with 5 Brains Then Make Up Your Mind, and How to Lie and Get Away with It Every Time.

Drawing from real-life experiences, psychological principles, and strategic thinking, Anthony's work provides readers with the tools they need to break free from self-doubt, silence their inner and outer critics, and navigate life's toughest challenges with confidence.

"There are no problems in life... only issues and answers."

Through his writing, Anthony challenges readers to rethink their past, redefine their future, and approach decision-making with clarity and purpose.
