Schools

Fixer Nation Positivity, Health & Wellness Network partners with schools to support student wellness, engagement, resilience, and readiness to learn. Our programs are designed to complement the work already being done by educators by reinforcing positive habits, encouraging a growth mindset, and helping students build the life and decision-making skills that support both academic and personal success.

We work with elementary, middle, high school, and higher education institutions to provide age-appropriate programming that focuses on mindset development, responsibility, decision-making, and preparation for life beyond the classroom.

Our goal is simple: to support schools in developing confident, capable, and resilient students who are prepared not just for graduation, but for life.
