Meet the Fixer

Anthony J. Placito

Bestselling author Anthony J. Placito draws on real-life experience, psychological insight, and strategic thinking to help readers silence self-doubt, redefine their future, and navigate life's toughest moments with clarity and purpose.

His books — Your Past Doesn't Define You, Kill the Bully, Think with 5 Brains, and How to Lie and Get Away With It Every Time — form the foundation of the Fixer Nation philosophy.
