Parents' Bill of Rights & Transparency Compliance

How Fixer Nation Education ensures complete curriculum visibility, data privacy, and alignment with state and national parental rights standards.

The National Baseline Standard

Every Fixer Nation Education classroom — in every state — operates under the same three commitments, regardless of local law. State-specific requirements below build on top of this baseline; they never weaken it.

👁️

Curriculum Transparency

Every daily Morning Boost video, script, and family-facing handout is previewable by school administrators, teachers, and parents before it's ever assigned. Teacher copies, lesson plans, and quiz answer keys stay limited to the licensed educator — so answer keys are never exposed to the students being assessed.

🔒

Data Privacy & Security

Student quiz responses and written reflections are visible only to that student's own teacher, by design — not even a linked parent account can see them. Parents instead see lesson completion status for their child. Everything is role-restricted and encrypted in transit.

🌱

Character-Focused Foundations

Our daily SEL content focuses on universally recognized civic virtues — resilience, responsibility, empathy, and decision-making — keeping lessons focused on positive character development rather than any contested or political subject matter.
