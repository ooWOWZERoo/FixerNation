For Schools & Districts

A schoolwide SEL routine administrators can support and teachers can actually sustain.

Fixer Nation Education (FNE) is a licensed 2D SEL platform — 2D meaning digital delivery — that brings social-emotional learning, character, and life skills to every classroom, every school day. Teachers can deliver lessons through the FNE Digital Classroom, present them on existing classroom displays, or print supporting materials, giving your school flexibility without sacrificing consistency. Administrators get real-time visibility; teachers get a morning routine they can actually use.

Institutional Benefits

What FNE delivers at the school level

Consistent daily SEL is a structural benefit for the whole building. FNE gives every classroom a shared framework and shared language.

01

Consistent Daily Delivery

Every teacher in the building has access to the same new lesson every school morning, creating a consistent daily SEL moment across classrooms, not just where one teacher champions it.

02

Common Language Across Classrooms

Students hear the same Issues-to-Answers framework repeated throughout the day and across grade levels. Shared vocabulary builds the habits that isolated lessons cannot.

03

Implementation Visibility

The School Admin dashboard shows which teachers are assigning lessons, which classrooms are completing them, and where adoption is lagging, without surfacing individual student data.

04

Centralized License Management

The school administrator assigns teacher seats, monitors usage, and manages the school's license from a single dashboard. No per-teacher billing, no spreadsheet tracking.

Getting Started

From proposal to first lesson, in a few steps.

1

Select a License Plan

Choose Individual, School, or District, or contact us for a custom scope and proposal.

2

Complete Purchase or PO

Pay by card or submit a purchase order. PO-based schools receive an invoice for approval.

3

License Activated

Once payment is confirmed, your school's license is activated and your admin account is created.

4

Admin Configures the School

The school admin sets up the building in the admin portal and assigns seats to teachers.

5

Teachers Register

Teachers use their school email on the Teacher Registration page to verify eligibility and create accounts.

6

Classrooms Created, Students Enrolled

Teachers create Digital Classrooms and invite students via class code. First lesson can run the same day.
