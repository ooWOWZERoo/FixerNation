Welcome to the Fixer Nation demo

Thanks for taking a look. This demo is a working copy of Fixer Nation Education with sample classrooms, students and lessons. Everything in it is made up, so click around as much as you like.

How to explore

Each role has its own sign-in. Start as a teacher, then see what a student and a parent see, and finish with the school and district views.

Good to know

  • This demo is yours alone. Other groups work in their own copies and can't see what you do here, and we clear yours when your demo ends.
  • You can view every lesson, but downloads and uploads are turned off.
  • The demo can't send email. Invitations only work with example.com addresses, like teacher2@example.com.
  • Please don't enter real student information.
