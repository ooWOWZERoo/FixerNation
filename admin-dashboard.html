Recommended Insights To Track Next

  • Month-over-month growth: compare this month's sales to last month's to spot momentum or a slowdown early.
  • Quote-to-sale conversion rate: of the schools who requested a formal quote, what share became a paying license customer?
  • Revenue by category: books vs. single-teacher licenses vs. school/group licenses — which line is actually driving growth.
  • Days sales outstanding: average time between an invoice being issued and marked paid, to catch slow-paying schools early.
  • Customer lifetime value: average total spend per contact over their full history, to see if repeat/renewal purchases are happening.
  • Refund/cancellation rate: share of invoices marked cancelled, as a data quality and sales-process health signal.
