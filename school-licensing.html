FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we start with only a few teachers?
Yes. Many schools begin with a pilot group and expand over time as more teachers adopt the curriculum. There's no minimum commitment. You can start with a single teacher license and add more seats at any time.
Can we add licenses later?
Absolutely. Additional licenses can be purchased at any time. Your School License Administrator can request more seats directly through the admin portal as new staff join your organization.
What happens if all licenses are already assigned?
Teachers attempting to register will be informed that no licenses are currently available. They may either purchase an individual license or request that the School License Administrator be notified to obtain additional licenses. This notification is sent automatically through the registration flow.
Does every teacher need their own account?
Yes. Each teacher receives an individual account linked to your school's license so progress, curriculum access, and reporting remain accurate for your organization. Accounts are created using each teacher's school-issued email address.
Who manages our licenses?
Your designated School License Administrator manages teacher invitations, license assignments, and license availability through the Fixer Nation Education Admin Portal. You can designate one or more administrators for your organization.
How long does implementation take?
Most schools can begin onboarding teachers within one business day after licensing is finalized. The teacher registration process takes just a few minutes. Teachers enter their school email, create an account, and gain immediate access to the curriculum.
