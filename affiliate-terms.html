Affiliate program terms.

1. What the program is

Affiliates bring Fixer Nation Education to schools and districts. You get a personal referral link, and you earn commission on license purchases made through it.

2. Applying and approval

A person here reads every application, and we may decline one. Approval isn't guaranteed. If you are approved, we confirm your commission rate and territory with you, send your referral link, and tell you how and when commissions are paid.

3. Your referral link and credit

When someone arrives on your link, it saves a referral cookie in their browser for 90 days. A purchase made in that time is credited to you. If the same person later arrives on a different affiliate's link, the newer link gets the credit.

A buyer who clears their cookies, switches browsers or devices, or never uses your link can't be credited to you. Our records decide who gets credit for an order.

4. Territories

Each territory is one state plus one county, and a county normally has one active affiliate. We may also give an affiliate a whole state, and when we do, some of its counties can be shared with other affiliates. Territory never decides who earns a commission. You can ask for a territory on your account page, and every request needs our approval. We may confirm your request, suggest a different territory, or change a territory later if we need to.

5. Commissions

Your dashboard shows each sale and what it paid:

  • Pending: not confirmed yet.
  • Owed: confirmed and waiting to be paid.
  • Paid: already sent to you.

Commission on an order that is refunded, cancelled or never paid can be reversed. Commissions on orders that were paid and not refunded stay yours, even if the program or your participation ends later. You are responsible for any taxes on what you earn.

6. Playing fair

  • Describe Fixer Nation Education honestly, and don't promise results, prices or terms we haven't given you. Prices come from the quotes we send.
  • Don't present yourself as an FNE employee, or say a school or district endorses us when it hasn't.
  • Don't send spam or unwanted bulk email.
  • Follow the rules schools and districts have about vendors, gifts and purchasing.
  • Use our name and logo only the way we give them to you.

7. Your role

You are an independent partner. You aren't an FNE employee or agent, and you can't sign anything or make commitments on our behalf.

8. Privacy

We keep your application, territory and payout records to run the program. The referral cookie is described in our Privacy Policy.

9. Ending the program or your participation

You can stop at any time by telling us. We can end your participation if you break these terms, and we may change or end the program. We will tell you by email if we do.

10. Changes and contact

We may update these terms, and the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions can go through the Contact page or Get help. The Terms of Use also apply to your use of the site.
