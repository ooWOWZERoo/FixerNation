Your Privacy Choices

Fixer Nation does not sell or share your personal information with third parties, and we don't use third-party advertising trackers on this site. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and how to reach us if you want to see, delete, or opt out of any of it.

What we actually collect

We keep this simple on purpose — there's no data broker relationship or ad network behind this site to account for.

  • Information you give us directly — your name, email, and whatever you write in a form like this one, Ask The Fixer, or a license quote request. Used only to respond to you or fulfill an order.
  • Anonymous visit data — which pages get viewed and in what order, with no name, email, or IP address attached. It's stored in your browser tab only (not a persistent cookie) and clears automatically when you close it.

Opt out of anonymous visit tracking

Even though this data never includes your name or IP address, you can turn it off entirely on this device.
