About the Fixer Nation Morning Boost Lesson Plan Library

The Fixer Nation Morning Boost Lesson Plan Library covers SEL, character, life skills, wellness, and personal growth, with new lessons released every weekday. Each one comes with a video, discussion guide, quiz, answer key, classroom poster, and teacher materials, ready to go.

Most SEL programs give teachers a fixed set of lessons to work through a few times a year. This library works differently: a fresh lesson every school day, built on the same Issues-to-Answers framework, so the practice compounds rather than resets.
