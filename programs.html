Program Catalog

SEL, character, and life skills — for every grade and every morning.

Fixer Nation Education (FNE) delivers a new Morning Boost lesson every school day, organized across 8 program themes that span the full 2D SEL spectrum. Every lesson covers all grade levels: Elementary, Middle, and High School.

2D SEL Program Themes

Eight dimensions. One daily routine.

2D SEL means digital delivery of social-emotional learning: character, resilience, decision-making, wellness, and life skills. Morning Boost lessons cycle through all eight themes throughout the school year.
