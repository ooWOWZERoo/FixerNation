Teacher Path

From browsing the curriculum to getting full access — here's how it works.

1

Browse the lesson plan library

Start at the National Education Portal to see the full Morning Boost SEL curriculum — 70+ lesson plans covering mindset, responsibility, wellness, and more. Each lesson includes a classroom poster, objectives, activities, and a quiz with answer key.

Open the Education Portal →
2

Check if your school is already licensed

Your school may already have a group license. Go to the 2D Education — Schools page and enter your school-issued email address. We'll instantly check whether a seat is available for you under your school's license.

Check My Eligibility →
3

If a seat is available — create your account

If your school has an active license with open seats, you'll be prompted to create a Fixer Nation Education account using your school email. Once verified, your access is immediate.

Register Now →
4

If no seat is available — you have two options

You can notify your school administrator directly from the registration page — they'll be alerted that a teacher needs an additional license seat. Or, you can purchase an individual teacher license instantly without waiting.

Buy an Individual License →
5

Access the full curriculum

Once your account is active and your license is confirmed, log in to access all lesson plans, downloadable resources, classroom posters, and quizzes — new content added every weekday.

Go to the Lesson Library →
🏫

School Administrator Path

License the 2D SEL program for your entire staff — most schools are up and running within one business day.

1

Learn what's included in a school license

Visit the School Licensing page to understand exactly what your school gets: access for every teacher, a dedicated admin portal, support for all grade levels (Elementary through High School), and district-wide options. Browse pricing or request a formal proposal — no commitment required.

View School Licensing →
2

Request a proposal or purchase directly

Fill out the inquiry form on the School Licensing page with your school's details — number of teachers, grade levels, and your implementation timeline. We'll respond with a customized proposal. If you're ready to purchase now, you can do so directly using a credit card or purchase order.

Request a Proposal →
3

Complete your purchase

Choose the payment method that works for your school — credit card (Stripe), purchase order, or a formal invoice. Purchase Orders are accepted and license access is granted immediately so your teachers aren't waiting on your business office to process payment.

See License Options →
4

Set up your administrator portal

Once your license is active, you'll receive access to the School License Admin Portal. From there you can invite teachers, assign license seats, monitor who has registered, add additional seats as your program grows, and manage your entire staff roster — all in one place.

Open Admin Portal →
5

Teachers register — you're done

Share the 2D Education — Schools page with your staff. Teachers enter their school email to check eligibility, create their account, and gain immediate access to the full curriculum. If a teacher can't find a seat, they can notify you directly from the registration page so you know to add more seats.

Teacher Registration Page →
