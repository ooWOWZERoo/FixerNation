Why Parents Love FNE

More than a window into homework

FNE covers the skills school report cards don't — grit, empathy, self-regulation, conflict resolution. Now you can see exactly what your child is working on and pick up where the teacher leaves off.

👀
See every lesson your child takes
Your parent portal shows the exact lessons assigned to your child's class — the topic, the skills covered, and when they completed it. No more "nothing" when you ask what happened at school.
💬
Conversation starters that actually work
Each lesson comes with a take-home discussion guide. Real questions, tied to what your kid just learned, that get more than a shrug at the dinner table.
📈
Progress you can actually read
Track quiz scores, lesson completion, and skill growth over time. You'll see what's clicking and where your child could use more support — before the semester ends.
🔬
Content built on real research
Every FNE lesson is grounded in published SEL research and reviewed by educators before it reaches a classroom. You don't have to take anyone's word for it — the research is cited right in the lesson materials.
🏠
Tools to reinforce skills at home
Access parent-facing lesson summaries, activity ideas, and printable resources so the SEL work doesn't stop when the school day does.
🔒
Age-appropriate, educator-reviewed
All content is reviewed by licensed educators and school counselors before publishing. Nothing gets through that isn't appropriate for the grade level.

Getting Started

How parents get access

1
Your child's teacher or school invites you
Your school's FNE administrator sends a parent invite to the email address on file. Check with your child's teacher if you haven't received one.
2
Set up your account in under two minutes
Click the link in the invite email, set a password, and your portal is ready. No app to download.
3
Log in anytime from any device
The parent portal works on phone, tablet, or desktop. Check in whenever you want to see what's going on in class.
4
Use the take-home guides at dinner
Each lesson's discussion guide gives you five conversation starters matched to exactly what your child learned that week.
