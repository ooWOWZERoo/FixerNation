1. Who we are and what these terms cover

Fixer Nation Education ("FNE," "we," "us") is run by Fixer Nation Issues and Answers. When you use the site or buy a license, you agree to these terms. If you are acting for a school or district, you are telling us you have the authority to accept them for that organization.

If you don't agree with something here, please don't use the site, and tell us what bothers you through the Contact page.

2. Who can use FNE

  • Teachers and school or district administrators, under a license.
  • Parents, once a child's teacher sends an invitation.
  • Students, through a classroom account their teacher sets up. Students don't sign up on their own and don't need an email address.
  • Anyone, to read the public pages and play the public Brain Games.

Accounts for teachers, administrators and parents are for adults.

3. Your account

Give us accurate information and keep your password private. Each teacher needs their own account, so please don't share a login with a colleague. If you think someone else got into your account, tell us right away through Get help.

We can suspend an account that breaks these terms or puts other people at risk. You can ask us to close your account at any time.

4. Licenses

A license lets the teachers it covers use FNE lessons, videos, handouts and games in their own classrooms during the license term. There are three kinds: an Individual Educator license for one teacher, a School License for a building, and a District License for several schools.

A license works through its end date. The night after that date it is marked expired, teacher seats switch off and those teachers are signed out. Classrooms, rosters and lesson picks are kept, and the seats that expiry switched off come back when the license is renewed.

Licenses don't renew on their own. We send renewal reminders before the end date.

A license can't be sold, lent or transferred to another school or person. If you accepted a quote or proposal that says something different from these terms about your order, the quote controls for that order.

5. Orders and payment

School and district pricing comes from a quote. A quote can be accepted until the end of its valid-until date, and what it lists (seats, term and total) is what you pay. Individual teacher licenses are priced on the licenses page.

You can pay by card or by purchase order. Card payments happen on Stripe's secure page, and we never see or store your card number. With a purchase order, we send an invoice, and the license stays pending until we receive payment confirmation. Teachers can be invited once the license opens.

If your organization is tax-exempt, send us a request through Get help and tell us which form you need. Refunds and cancellations are covered on the Refunds and cancellations page.

6. What you can do with our content

FNE and the people who license material to us own the lessons, videos, scripts, handouts, games, logos and site design. Your license gives you a limited right to use them for teaching, or for a parent to use with their own child. It does not give you ownership.

Print and share handouts with your own students and their families. Please don't post lessons, videos, teacher copies or answer keys where the public can reach them, resell them, or hand them to people outside your license. Teacher copies and answer keys are for licensed educators only.

7. How to behave

Please don't:

  • try to get around security, probe the site for weaknesses, or overload it
  • use another person's account, or collect data from the site with automated tools
  • upload malware, or anything unlawful, harassing, or harmful to children
  • put someone else's personal information into a reflection, goal or community post
  • pretend to be someone you are not

Students write short reflections and goals in their lessons, and we check that writing. If something serious comes up, an alert email goes to the people the school chose. Community posts are visible only to verified members, and we may remove a post that doesn't fit these rules.

8. Safety, and what FNE is not

FNE is an educational program for social-emotional learning. It isn't counseling, therapy, medical care or legal advice, and it isn't a crisis service. If someone may be in danger, call 911 in the United States or call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, and tell a trusted adult at school.

We check student writing and send alert emails when something serious appears, but we can't promise a person is watching at every hour. Schools stay responsible for supervising their students.
