How the Program Use Report counts

This page explains every number in the Program Use Report that Fixer Nation Education gives to district and school administrators, what it leaves out, and how small groups are protected. You are welcome to share it with a board, a state office or a funder.

What the report is

The report describes how much Fixer Nation is being used in a district's schools during the current school year, and which New York social emotional learning benchmarks and graduate attributes students have practiced. Every figure is a count at school level. No teacher, classroom or student is named, and there is no view of any one student.

This report describes how the program is being used. It is not an evaluation of student outcomes.

What each number means

  • Teachers activated. Teacher seats that have been registered, out of the seats the school has licensed. School and district administrators do not use a seat.
  • Students enrolled. Students a teacher has in a class on Fixer Nation. Students a teacher has removed are left out.
  • Active student. A student who finished at least one lesson, or finished a game (assigned or free play), in the period. Starting something does not count, and Fixer Nation does not track logins.
  • Reach. Active students as a share of students enrolled, over the school year.
  • Activities finished. Each student's finished activities, counted once per activity per month, so the months add up to the school year. A student who plays the same game again in a later month counts again in that month.
  • Classroom in regular use. A classroom where students finished activities in at least three different weeks of the month. Staff can adjust the number of weeks.
  • Needs support. A school mark, shown when fewer than half of its classrooms were in regular use in the last full month. It points to where an offer of help may be useful. It is not a rating.
  • Standards coverage. The share of students who have practiced each benchmark or attribute. A student has practiced one once they finish an activity that Fixer Nation connects to it. An attribute with no connected activity says so, instead of showing zero. New York's social emotional learning benchmarks are shown under the three goals the state uses.
  • The school year runs 1 August to 31 July. The report shows the current year, month by month, and last year from totals saved each month. Totals for earlier months are filled in from classroom activity when the report is first switched on, so last year can have a few months filled in that way.

How small groups are protected

  • Schools with fewer than a set number of enrolled students (10 by default) are combined into one row, so no student can be picked out.
  • A school with fewer than a set number of activated teachers (5 by default) hides its teacher and classroom figures. A figure such as "1 of 2 classrooms" would point at a person.
  • A district with fewer enrolled students than the minimum shows no figures at all.
  • In a download, a figure that is hidden says suppressed. It is never shown as 0.

What this report cannot tell you

  • Outcomes. Measures such as attendance, behavior referrals or climate surveys come from your own data. Fixer Nation does not hold them.
  • Results by student group. Fixer Nation holds no demographic information about students, so figures cannot be split by group.
  • Rating levels for the state's rubric. New York's sample implementation rubric is a voluntary self-assessment for a district to complete. These counts can support your own self-assessment, but Fixer Nation does not produce rubric levels.
  • An individual student, teacher or classroom. The report is built so that it cannot show one.

Following New York's guidance on the SEL benchmarks, nothing here is used to diagnose or grade students, and there is no per-student view.

About the standards

Fixer Nation's connections to the New York frameworks are its own mapping. New York State and CASEL have not endorsed or certified them. Each connection is checked by a person before it counts, and the date and source of each framework are shown in the report.
